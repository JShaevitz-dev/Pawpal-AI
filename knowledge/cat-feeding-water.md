Adult cats are often fed two or more measured meals a day. Many cats prefer several small meals, and
puzzle feeders can spread a meal out and add mental exercise. Kittens need more frequent meals.

Cats tend not to drink much, so fresh water should be easy to find. Refresh the water daily. Some cats
drink more from a wide shallow bowl or a fountain, and wet food also adds water to the diet.

Keep food and water away from the litter box; many cats will not eat or drink near it.

A cat that stops eating for more than a day or so should be seen by a vet, because cats can become
seriously ill quickly when they do not eat.
