How often a dog needs brushing depends on the coat. Long or double coats benefit from brushing several
times a week to prevent mats; short coats can be brushed about once a week to remove loose hair.

Brushing a dog's teeth daily is ideal and a few times a week still helps. Use toothpaste made for dogs,
never human toothpaste, because some contain xylitol, which is toxic to dogs.

Nails usually need trimming every three to six weeks. If you can hear the nails clicking on a hard floor,
they are probably too long.

Most dogs only need to bathe every one to three months, or when they get dirty. Bathing too often can dry
out the skin.
