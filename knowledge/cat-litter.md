Scoop the litter box at least once a day. Cats are clean animals and may start going outside the box if
it is dirty. Replace all of the litter and wash the box with mild soap every one to two weeks, more often
for clumping-free litter.

A common guideline is one litter box per cat plus one extra, placed in quiet spots the cat can reach
easily.

Changes in how often a cat uses the box, straining, or crying in the box can be signs of a medical
problem. A male cat that strains and produces little or no urine needs a vet urgently.
