Give medication exactly as your vet prescribed: the same dose, at the times they told you, for the full
course. Do not change a dose, stop early, or give human medicine to a pet without talking to your vet
first. Many common human medicines, including ibuprofen and acetaminophen, are dangerous to pets.

Medication with a set schedule should be a high-priority task at a fixed time so it is never the thing
that gets dropped on a busy day. If a dose is missed, call your vet or check the label instead of
doubling the next dose.

Monthly preventives such as flea, tick and heartworm treatments are easy to forget. A recurring reminder
on the same day each month helps.
