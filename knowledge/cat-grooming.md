Short-haired cats groom themselves well but still benefit from a weekly brush, which reduces shedding and
hairballs. Long-haired cats usually need brushing every day or two to prevent mats.

Trim the sharp tips of a cat's claws every two to three weeks if the cat does not wear them down on a
scratching post. Never cut into the pink quick.

Dental disease is common in cats. Brushing with cat toothpaste is the most effective home care; dental
treats can help a little. Bad breath or drooling is worth mentioning to your vet.
