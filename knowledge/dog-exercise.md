Dogs need daily physical activity. Many adult dogs need somewhere between 30 minutes and 2 hours a day,
depending on breed, age and health. High-energy working breeds sit at the top of that range; small,
senior or short-nosed dogs often need less but still benefit from regular short walks.

Splitting exercise into two or more walks is usually better than one long outing. A morning walk also
doubles as a bathroom break, and most adult dogs need a chance to relieve themselves at least three to
five times a day.

Signs a dog is not getting enough exercise include chewing, digging, barking and restlessness in the
evening. Signs of too much include lagging behind, limping or heavy panting long after the walk ends.
