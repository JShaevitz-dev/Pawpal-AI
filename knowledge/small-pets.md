Rabbits and guinea pigs need unlimited fresh hay every day; it should make up most of their diet.
A small measured amount of pellets and a portion of fresh leafy greens round it out. Guinea pigs also
need vitamin C from their food because they cannot make their own.

Spot-clean the enclosure daily and do a full clean weekly. Small pets also need daily time outside the
cage or in a larger exercise area.

Needs vary a lot between species, so ask a vet who treats exotic pets about your specific animal.
