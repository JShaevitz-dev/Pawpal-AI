Indoor cats need daily play to stay fit and to keep from getting bored. Two or three short sessions of interactive
play, about 10 to 15 minutes each, work well for most cats. Wand toys that let a cat stalk, chase and
pounce copy natural hunting behavior.

Ending a play session with a "catch" and then a small meal or treat fits the cat's hunt, catch, eat,
groom, sleep rhythm and helps the cat settle.

If you use a laser pointer, finish on a toy the cat can actually catch so the game does not end in
frustration. Scratching posts, window perches and puzzle feeders give enrichment when you are not home.
