Most adult dogs do well on two meals a day, roughly 8 to 12 hours apart. Puppies usually need three or
four smaller meals a day. Feeding at about the same times each day makes digestion and house training
more predictable, so a feeding task is a good candidate for a set time.

Measure food with a cup or scale rather than guessing. The amount on the food label is a starting point;
the right amount depends on the dog's size, age and activity, and your vet can tell you a target weight.

Fresh water should always be available. Rinse and refill the water bowl at least once a day, and wash
food and water bowls regularly because they build up a slimy film.

Avoid feeding a large meal right before or right after hard exercise. Waiting about an hour is a common
rule of thumb, especially for large, deep-chested breeds.
